# Harbor 4.2 — connection pooling
Pooled egress connections, cutting p99 egress from 32 ms to 20 ms and retiring the per-request handshake.

The [[Harbor Latency Budget|latency budget]]'s 20 ms egress line was written against this release; before 4.2 the hop was budgeted at 55 ms and routinely missed.
