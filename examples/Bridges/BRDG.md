# BRDG
The machines this host can reach — a small, flat collection.

> [!note] Canonical curated spine
> A hub whose children are **listed by hand, one row each**, with the `...` kept as a safety valve rather than as the content.
> - **The catchall should stay nearly empty.** Every machine here has an authored row; the `...` exists so that a fifth machine added tomorrow still shows up instead of vanishing silently.
> - **It is not a [[HHOP|list spine]].** These rows are hand-written *above* the marker; a list spine's rows are machine-written *below* a `---`. Same visual result, opposite ownership — and only the machine-written kind stays correct on its own.
> - **It is not [[HRUN|grouped]] either**, because four machines do not fall into cohesive groups. Grouped is the special case of curated where real groupings exist; inventing them here would add structure the material does not have.
>
> The live counterpart is [[Legal]] — about a hundred hand-listed contracts and invoices that genuinely do not sort into tidy piles.
